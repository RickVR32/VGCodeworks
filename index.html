FAQ

Good Questions

Will coding void my warranty?

Enabling factory features generally doesn't, but a manufacturer can decline a claim if they believe a change caused a fault. Coding is fully reversible, and I back up your original configuration so it can be restored before a dealer visit if you want.

Do I need to tell my insurer?

For a remap or a hardware retrofit, yes. Insurers class these as modifications and not declaring them can invalidate a policy. Most cosmetic or convenience coding changes aren't modifications, but if you're unsure, ask your insurer.

How do I know what my car supports?

Send your reg or VIN through the form. I'll check the factory build and fitted hardware and come back with a list of what's possible on your specific car.

Where do you work?

Mobile service from Orpington covering Bromley, South East London and nearby Kent. I need a safe place to park and, for longer jobs, ideally off-road. Further afield is possible, just ask.

Can a remap be reversed?

Yes. Your original software is read and saved before anything is written, and the car can be returned to stock.

Do you do DPF, EGR or cat deletes?

No. They're illegal on road cars in the UK and will fail an MOT. If your DPF or EGR is giving trouble, I can diagnose it properly so it gets fixed rather than removed.
