FAQ

Before You Book

How can I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna through Stripe's secure checkout. Apple Pay and Google Pay appear automatically on devices that support them. Klarna lets you pay in 3 interest-free instalments or pay in 30 days, subject to Klarna's approval. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch this site.

What's included in the Full Unlock?

Every software coding tweak your specific car supports, done in one visit. It covers coding only, so hardware retrofits are quoted separately. Send your reg first and I'll list what's possible on your car.

What tools do you use?

Diagnostics and coding are done with VCDS, the go-to diagnostic and coding software for VW Group cars. A few newer systems are locked and need dealer-only tools; if your job needs one, I'll tell you before you pay.

How do discount codes work?

Enter your code above before you book, or add it on the checkout page. It's checked and applied when you pay. For quoted jobs, add the code to your enquiry and it'll be taken off your quote.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Reschedule for free with at least 24 hours' notice. You can cancel for a full refund any time before the work starts. If I've already bought non-returnable parts for your job, I may keep their cost. Full details are in the terms.

Will coding void my warranty?

Enabling factory features generally doesn't, but a manufacturer can decline a claim if they believe a change caused a fault. Coding is fully reversible and your original configuration is backed up, so it can be restored before a dealer visit.

Do I need to tell my insurer?

For a remap or a hardware retrofit, yes. Insurers class these as modifications and not declaring them can invalidate a policy. Most convenience coding isn't a modification, but if you're unsure, ask your insurer.

What do I need to have ready?

The car, both keys if possible, and a safe place to park. For longer jobs, off-road parking and access to the car for the full session is best.

Do you do DPF, EGR or cat deletes?

No. They're illegal on road cars in the UK and will fail an MOT. If your DPF or EGR is giving trouble, I can diagnose it properly so it gets fixed rather than removed.
